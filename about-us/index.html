Who we areWho we are

Schrauben Technologies is a leading industrial distributor and service provider, specializing in fasteners, high-precision machined components, and end-to-end supply chain solutions. We empower manufacturers across industries to enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and improve overall profitability.

We go beyond distribution — we offer Total Supply Management, built on deep expertise in supplier selection, planning, and execution, and backed by proprietary logistics systems that optimize the physical flow of materials.

Our services are designed for world-class international manufacturers across diverse industry segments. Whether it’s ensuring consistent quality, reducing lead times, or managing complex multi-tier supplier networks — Schrauben Technologies acts as your strategic partner, delivering point-of-use, cross-industry solutions on a global scale.
