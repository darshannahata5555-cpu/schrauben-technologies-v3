Threaded InsertsThreaded Inserts

A bolt is an externally threaded part which is used along with a nut to hold two or more non threaded parts. We can supply various types of cold/hot forged metric/Inch series standard bolts with different shape, size, material and finish. We can also supply special bolts as per customer drawings/specific requirements.
