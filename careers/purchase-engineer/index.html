Purchase Engineer (4-6 Years Experience)Purchase Engineer (4-6 Years Experience)

Job Description:

  • Location: Alandi Phata, Pune.
  • Job Type: Full-Time.
  • Industry: Manufacturing / Engineering
  • Salary: Competitive, based on experience.
  • Required Candidate Immediate Joiner to 15 Days Notice Period.

Key Responsibilities:

  1. Develop and implement purchasing strategies aligned with company goals.
  2. Identify, evaluate, and manage suppliers to ensure timely and cost-effective procurement of materials.
  3. Negotiate contracts, terms, and pricing with vendors.
  4. Collaborate with engineering, production, and quality teams to understand technical requirements.
  5. Monitor inventory levels and forecast future supply needs.
  6. Ensure compliance with company policies and industry regulations.
  7. Analyze market trends and provide input for budget planning and supplier selection.
  8. Resolve supplier issues and maintain long-term vendor relationships.

Qualifications & Experience:

  1. Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or a related field.
  2. 4–6 years of experience in a purchase or procurement of Fasteners within a manufacturing or engineering environment.
  3. Strong understanding of supply chain processes and sourcing strategies.
  4. Excellent negotiation and communication skills.
  5. Proficient in ERP systems and Microsoft Office Suite.
  6. Detail-oriented with strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Skills:

  1. Knowledge of international sourcing and import procedures.
  2. Experience with quality management systems (ISO, Six Sigma, etc.).
  3. Certification in Supply Chain Management or Procurement (CIPS, CPSM, etc.) is a plus.
